v1.1.0 "Upgrades" Update

Added: • Autocrafter Core (crafts items in your whitelist if the materials are in the hub chest).
• Keychain Module (lets you add 9 more hub keys to a sorter — right click with the upgrade in hand to open).
• Stack Booster (increases the stack number of transferred items).
• Speed Coil (increases the speed at which items get transferred).
• Note: Speed Coil and Stack Booster are stackable up to 8 per stack to increase the effectiveness of the upgrade.
