v1.0.0 Initial Release

Added: • Sorrow system that builds over time while cursed.
• Environmental decay effects that intensify as sorrow rises.
• Light sources (torches, lanterns, etc.) can flicker or dim when sorrow is high.
• Villagers react to cursed players by stepping back instead of taking damage.
• Passive mobs take damage over time near high-sorrow players.
• Bandages that suppress sorrow growth (temporary relief, not a cure).
• Golden Bandages that allow solo cleansing (ritual still required).
• Cleansing ritual at midnight using Crying Obsidian + a wooden altar + candles.
