📖 How Lore Works

A story that unlocks through play

EchoBloom's lore isn't something you read once — it's something you discover. Every realm has a persistent Book of Lore page in Discord that updates live as new content is unlocked.

🌀 Waves

Lore is organized into waves. Each wave contains 5 chapters and 3 fragments per realm. When all four realms complete a wave's chapters, the Convergence event triggers: realm progress resets, level caps increase, and the next wave begins. Lore from past waves is preserved and viewable with /wave-history.

📖 Chapters

Each realm has 5 lore chapters per wave. They unlock when the realm reaches milestone levels. Wave 1 milestones: 3, 7, 12, 18, 25. Wave 2 milestones: 3, 8, 14, 20, 28. When a chapter unlocks, it's announced in the realm's lore channel and the Book of Lore page updates automatically.

🧩 Fragments

Lore fragments are rare collectibles that drop from normal conversation (1% chance per XP-eligible message). Each realm has 3 fragments per wave at different rarities — uncommon, rare, and legendary. They reveal deeper truths about each realm's nature and appear on the Book of Lore page when found.

🎒 Items & Crafting

Items drop from messages and belong to the realm's shared inventory. Each realm has 6 unique items (2 common, 2 uncommon, 1 rare, 1 legendary), plus 8 universal items any realm can find. Common and uncommon items can be combined through the crafting system to create powerful usable items. Items can also be gifted or traded between realms.

⚔️ Realm Battles & Raids

Events that trigger from conversation

As members chat, there's a chance a realm event will trigger spontaneously (5% per XP-earning message, with a 1-hour cooldown between events). Admins can also start events manually. Six event types plus realm raids:

⚡ Riddle Battles — A riddle is posted. First realm member to answer correctly wins 500 XP for their realm.

🏃 Reaction Races — A target emoji appears. Fastest realm member to react wins 300 XP.

🔎 Item Hunts — A specific item is announced. The first realm to find it through boosted drops wins 400 XP.

🔤 Word Scrambles — A scrambled word with a hint. First to unscramble wins 400 XP.

🔥 XP Frenzies — A 10-minute window where all XP is doubled. The realm earning the most XP during the frenzy wins 300 bonus XP.

🔗 Emoji Chains — React in the correct sequence of 5 emojis. First to complete the chain wins 350 XP.

🏴 Realm Raids — Player-initiated via /raid. Attack another realm; power is based on participant levels, reaction count, and randomness. Winners steal 3 items.
