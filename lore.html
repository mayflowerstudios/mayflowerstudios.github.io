📖 How Lore Works

A story that unlocks through play

EchoBloom's lore isn't something you read once — it's something you discover. Every realm has a persistent Book of Lore page in Discord that updates live as new content is unlocked.

📖 Chapters

Each realm has 5 lore chapters. They unlock when the realm reaches milestone levels (3, 7, 12, 18, 25). When a chapter unlocks, it's announced in the realm's lore channel and the Book of Lore page updates automatically.

🧩 Fragments

Lore fragments are rare collectibles that drop from normal conversation (2.5% chance per message). Each realm has 3 fragments at different rarities — uncommon, rare, and legendary. They reveal deeper truths about each realm's nature and appear on the Book of Lore page when found.

🎒 Items

Items also drop from messages and belong to the realm's shared inventory. Each realm has unique items, plus universal items any realm can find. Items can be gifted or traded between realms.

⚔️ Realm Battles

Events that trigger from conversation

As members chat, there's a chance a realm event will trigger spontaneously (2% per XP-earning message, with a 4-hour cooldown between events). Admins can also start events manually. Three types:

⚡ Riddle Battles — A riddle is posted. First realm member to answer correctly wins XP for their realm.

🏃 Reaction Races — A target emoji appears. Fastest realm member to react wins.

🔎 Item Hunts — A specific item is announced. The first realm to find it through normal drops wins bonus XP.

The lore grows as the community grows. Every message, every chapter unlocked, every fragment found — it all builds the world together. Choose a realm and start discovering.
