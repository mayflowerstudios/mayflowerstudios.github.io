How it works

1
Set your name (top right), then send everyone the room link so you're all in the same room.
2
Either of you pastes a link and hits Load & Sync.
3
Anyone can play / pause / seek — it mirrors to everyone else, and shows who's hosting.
4
Drifted apart? Anyone can tap Resync to snap back together.
