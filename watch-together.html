How it works

1
Set your name (top right) and open this page on both devices — same room (it's cozy by default).
2
Either of you pastes a link and hits Load & Sync.
3
Either person can play / pause / seek — it mirrors to the other side, and shows who's hosting.
4
Drifted apart? Either of you taps Resync to snap back together.

Hop on a Discord call too if you want to chat while you watch 💬

Private rooms

Want a room that's just yours? Add ?room= and any name to the URL — for example watch-together.html?room=us. Share that exact link with each other and you'll meet there.

Tip: the Copy room link button copies your current room so you can send it over.
