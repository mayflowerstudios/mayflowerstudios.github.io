📝 Description

What EchoBloom is (in our server)

EchoBloom is our private "living world" bot. Members choose one of four realms and then build progress through daily conversation and voice chat: every message earns XP for both you and your realm, items drop randomly, lore fragments surface from the noise, and realm events can trigger at any moment.

On top of the realm layer sits a full class system — eight archetypes that shape how you earn XP, find items, contribute to your realm, and perform in combat. Pick a class, level it up through solo dungeon runs and boss fights, and prestige into a new class when you hit the cap to carry permanent stat bonuses forward. Master every class to earn the 🏆 Class Master title.

It's designed to feel like an RPG layer on top of chat — not a grindy spreadsheet. Progress happens naturally through conversation, with quests, crafting, raids, seasonal content, adventures, and competitive events adding texture over time. A wave system means the story keeps growing: when all realms complete a wave's lore, a Convergence event fires and the next chapter begins.

🌿 The Four Realms

Realm identity is a real system: roles, XP tracking, shared inventory, collective lore, and seasonal themes.

  • 🌿 The Grove of Growth — Earth · Renewal · Spring
  • 🔥 The Ember Circle — Fire · Transformation · Summer
  • 💧 The Mirror Lake — Water · Reflection · Autumn
  • 🌙 The Moonroot Tree — Spirit · Mystery · Winter

⭐ Core Systems

What makes EchoBloom EchoBloom

🌿 Realm Progression Core

Every message you send earns XP — for you and your realm. Both track levels independently, so individual effort lifts the whole community.

  • Join realms via a reaction-based realm message
  • Realm roles assigned automatically on react
  • Three rank milestones at Level 1, 10, and 25
  • Realm-wide and individual leaderboards
  • XP cooldown (60s) prevents spam — paces naturally
  • XP per message: 5–25 (15 base ± 10 variance)
  • Level caps scale per wave (base: 50 member / 30 realm)

🎙️ Voice XP Core

Earn XP just by hanging out in voice channels. No commands needed — just be present with at least one other person.

  • 5 XP per tick (every 5 minutes) while in voice
  • Minimum 2 non-bot members required in the channel
  • AFK channel and self-deafened users are excluded
  • Streak and item XP buffs apply to voice XP too
  • Scholar's Deep Focus and Warden's Ancient Bond stack voice XP bonuses

🔥 Streak System Core

Stay active daily and earn escalating XP bonuses. Streaks reward consistency without punishing breaks too harshly.

  • 7 days → +3% XP bonus
  • 14 days → +6% XP bonus
  • 21 days → +9% XP bonus
  • 28 days → +12% XP bonus (cap)
  • Sentinel's Rally, Wanderer's Road Rhythm, and Trickster's Lucky Escape add further streak bonuses
  • Check your streak anytime with /streak

⚔️ Class System New

Choose one of eight archetypes to shape how you earn, fight, and contribute. Your class is universal — any realm member can pick any class.

  • Pick once per Wave with /class pick
  • Three skills unlock at member levels 5, 15, and 30
  • Class level (1–10) earned through adventures and boss fights
  • Stats (Power, Wisdom, Agility, Vitality, Fortune) scale with class level
  • Prestige at Class Lv 10 → pick a new class, keep permanent stat bonuses
  • Play all 8 classes to earn 🏆 Class Master status
  • View your profile with /character

📜 Campaigns New

Long-form party adventures built from JSON files. A private channel is created for the group, scenes branch based on party votes, and every fight and item found carries forward to the finale.

  • Start a campaign with /campaign start — pick from a dropdown of adventures
  • Browse available campaigns with /campaign list
  • Party assembles publicly (2–6 players), then moves to a private channel
  • Five scene types: story, item, combat, boss, ending
  • Party votes on story choices — majority wins, 90-second window
  • HP, cooldowns, items, and buffs persist across every scene
  • Boss fights have phases, enrage timers, and XP scaled by damage dealt

⚔️ Realm Battles Event

Events trigger randomly from messages (5% chance, 1hr cooldown) or can be started by admins. Six battle types keep things fresh.

  • ⚡ Riddle Battles — first correct answer wins XP for their realm
  • 🏃 Reaction Races — fastest finger on the right emoji wins
  • 🔎 Item Hunts — first realm to find a target item claims the prize
  • 🔤 Word Scrambles — unscramble a word with a hint for realm XP
  • 🔥 XP Frenzies — 10-minute 2× XP window, winning realm gets bonus
  • 🔗 Emoji Chains — react in the correct sequence of 5 emojis

🏴 Realm Raids PvP

Launch a raid against another realm to steal their items. Power is based on participant levels, reaction count, and a dash of randomness.

  • Start a raid with /raid
  • Both sides rally members via reactions
  • Power = (avg level × 1.5) + (participants × 1.0) + random factor
  • Striker's Battle Charge and Invoker's Battle Fury boost raid damage contribution
  • Winners steal 3 items from the defending realm
  • 2-hour cooldown between raids
  • XP rewards for both sides (400 win / 100 lose)

🗡️ Rivalry PvP

Two targeted realm-vs-realm mechanics beyond full raids: Sabotage lets you sneak in and destroy items, while Challenge fires a server-wide trivia duel.

  • /sabotage <realm> — sneak into a rival realm and destroy some of their items
  • /challenge <realm> — challenge a rival realm to a trivia duel
  • Sabotage success depends on participant levels and a random roll
  • Trivia challenges are open to the whole server — first correct answer wins for their realm

📋 Quests Daily / Weekly

Quests rotate automatically — daily and weekly targets that reward bonus realm XP on completion.

  • Message count targets (e.g. "Send 50 messages today")
  • XP milestones (e.g. "Earn 500 XP today")
  • Item collection goals (e.g. "Find 2 items today")
  • Seasonal quests during active seasons
  • Progress tracked automatically from normal chatting

⚔️ Classes

Eight archetypes — choose wisely, prestige strategically

Classes are universal — any member of any realm can pick any class. Your class shapes your passive bonuses, the skills you unlock as you level up, and your stats in solo adventures and boss fights. You pick once per Wave. When you reach Class Level 10, you can prestige: switch to a new class and permanently carry 30% of your old class's max stats as a bonus. Play all 8 classes to earn 🏆 Class Master.

Class Level vs. Member Level

Member level comes from chatting — it unlocks class skills at levels 5, 15, and 30. Class level (1–10) is earned separately through /adventure and boss fights — it scales your stats and determines which adventure zones you can enter.

🗺️ Adventures & Boss Fights

Solo dungeons, group adventures, server boss fights, and class leveling

⚔️ Solo Adventures New

Run a solo dungeon with /adventure. Pick a zone, fight a monster round-by-round, and earn class XP and item drops on the way out.

  • 30-minute cooldown between runs
  • Pick your move each round — 12 seconds before it auto-resolves
  • Max 8 rounds per solo fight before the dungeon collapses
  • Combat uses Power, Agility, Vitality, Wisdom, and Fortune stats
  • Random events before combat: Shrines (restore HP), Ambushes (enemy first strike), Traps, Treasure Rooms (skip combat for guaranteed loot)
  • Earn class XP on any outcome; more on a full clear

👥 Group Adventures New

Team up for a shared dungeon run with /adventure_group. Invite friends, pick a zone, and fight together using the full group combat engine.

  • Uses the same zone list and class stats as solo adventures
  • Monster HP scales up with party size (+70% per extra member)
  • Party buffs, cooldowns, and HP carry between scenes
  • All participants earn class XP on completion
  • Same 30-minute cooldown as solo runs

Class XP is earned exclusively from adventures and boss fights. It's the only path to Class Level 10 and unlocking prestige.

📜 Lore, Waves & Seasons

The world that grows with you

📖 Realm Lore Story

Each realm has its own Book of Lore — a persistent page that updates live as new content is discovered.

  • 5 chapters per realm per wave, unlocking at realm level milestones
  • 3 lore fragments per realm per wave (uncommon / rare / legendary drops)
  • Fragment drop rate: 1% per XP-eligible message (boosted by Scholar, Warden, Oracle)
  • Each realm has its own dedicated lore channel
  • Lore pages auto-update when chapters unlock or fragments are found

🌀 Wave System Progression

Lore is organized into waves — each wave is a full set of chapters and fragments for every realm. When all realms complete a wave, the Convergence fires.

  • Wave 1: Origins — "Where it all began."
  • Wave 2: Echoes — "What the past left behind."
  • Convergence resets realm progress and begins the next wave
  • Lore from past waves is preserved in wave history
  • Level caps increase with each wave (+50 member / +10 realm)
  • Class choice resets each Wave — use /class pick at the start of each new wave
  • Track progress with /wave and /wave-history

🌸 Seasonal System Rotating

Four seasons, each tied to a realm. During a realm's season, exclusive content appears.

  • 🌱 Spring (Mar 20 – Jun 20) — Grove of Growth
  • ☀️ Summer (Jun 21 – Sep 22) — Ember Circle

  • ❄️ Winter (Dec 21 – Mar 19) — Moonroot Tree

📘 How to Use

Quick gameplay flow

1) Join a Realm

Pick a realm by reacting on the realm-selection message.

  • React with 🌿 🔥 💧 or 🌙 to join
  • EchoBloom assigns your realm role automatically
  • One realm at a time — unreact to leave
  • Non-realm emoji are blocked on the selection message

2) Pick a Class

Open the class guide, pick your archetype, and unlock your first passive bonus immediately.

  • /class info — compare all 8 classes before committing
  • /class pick — choose from a dropdown (once per Wave)
  • Your passive activates instantly — no level required
  • Skills unlock automatically at member levels 5, 15, and 30
  • Check your profile anytime with /character
  • Full Class Guide →

3) Chat & Progress

Just talk. Every message earns XP, drops items, and advances quests.

  • XP earned for you and your realm (5–25 per message, modified by class)
  • Voice chat earns XP too (5 per 5-min tick, boosted by Scholar / Warden)
  • Items drop randomly as you chat (boost by Wanderer / Trickster)
  • Lore fragments surface from conversation (1% chance, boosted by Scholar / Oracle / Warden)
  • Quests and streaks track automatically in the background

4) Run Adventures

Level up your class through solo dungeons and boss fights to unlock prestige.

  • /adventure — pick a zone and run a solo dungeon (30-min cooldown)
  • /adventure_group — invite friends for a group run
  • /class stats — check your current class level and stats before a run
  • Defeat the boss when one spawns for bonus class XP and shared rewards
  • Reach Class Lv 10 and use /class prestige to carry stat bonuses to a new class
  • Repeat until you've played all 8 classes to earn 🏆 Class Master

5) Track Progress

Check your realm, your rank, and how your realm stacks up.

  • /character — full profile: level, class, stats, skills, active bonuses
  • /realm — realm level, XP, rank, and progress bar
  • /streak — your daily streak and current XP bonus
  • /leaderboard — top members by Realm XP, Class Level, Boss Damage, or Adventures
  • /wave — current wave progress across all realms

6) Engage with Systems

Items, crafting, raids, quests, lore, events, seasons, campaigns, and adventures all layer on top of chat.

  • /inventory — view your realm's shared stash
  • /use — activate a usable item
  • /recipes and /craft — crafting system
  • /gift and /trade — move items between realms
  • /raid — attack another realm for their items
  • /sabotage and /challenge — targeted rivalry actions
  • /forge — fuse seasonal items into artifacts
  • /quests — view active daily/weekly/seasonal quests
  • /lore view — view your realm's story progress
  • /season — current season info and exclusive content
  • /campaign start — launch a long-form party adventure

🔧 Setup Notes

Start clean, keep it readable
Recommended rollout

Enable realms first. Once people are chatting and earning XP, introduce the class system — members will pick classes naturally once they understand the passives. Adventures and boss fights layer in cleanly after that since they don't require any channel setup. Items, quests, events, lore, and seasons all add texture over time. No need to introduce everything at once.

Setup flow: 1. Create realm roles (one per realm) 2. Create rank roles (3 per realm: Level 1, 10, 25) 3. Fill in role IDs in config/config.py 4. Run /setup realms in your realm-select channel 5. Create lore channels (one per realm) and run /setup lore 6. Set your events channel ID in config 7. Run /setup events to post the persistent dashboard 8. Run /setup wave to post the wave progress embed 9. Restart — quests auto-rotate, events trigger from chat 10. Run /season to verify seasonal content is active 11. Run /wave to check lore wave status 12. Announce /class pick to members — classes are ready out of the box 13. Adventures and boss fights are available immediately; no channel config needed
Everything is in one file

All IDs, realm definitions, items, crafting recipes, quests, lore waves, seasonal content, riddles, word scrambles, emoji chains, drop rates, adventure zones, monsters, and boss definitions live in config/config.py. One file to rule them all.
