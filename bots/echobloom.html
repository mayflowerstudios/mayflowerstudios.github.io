📝 Description

What EchoBloom is (in our server)

EchoBloom is our private "living world" bot. Members choose one of four realms and then build progress through daily conversation and voice chat: every message earns XP for both you and your realm, items drop randomly, lore fragments surface from the noise, and realm events can trigger at any moment.

It's designed to feel like an RPG layer on top of chat — not a grindy spreadsheet. Progress happens naturally through conversation, with quests, crafting, raids, seasonal content, and competitive events adding texture over time. A wave system means the story keeps growing: when all realms complete a wave's lore, a Convergence event fires and the next chapter begins.

🌿 The Four Realms

Realm identity is a real system: roles, XP tracking, shared inventory, collective lore, and seasonal themes.

  • 🌿 The Grove of Growth — Earth · Renewal · Spring
  • 🔥 The Ember Circle — Fire · Transformation · Summer
  • 💧 The Mirror Lake — Water · Reflection · Autumn
  • 🌙 The Moonroot Tree — Spirit · Mystery · Winter

⭐ Core Systems

What makes EchoBloom EchoBloom

🌿 Realm Progression Core

Every message you send earns XP — for you and your realm. Both track levels independently, so individual effort lifts the whole community.

  • Join realms via a reaction-based realm message
  • Realm roles assigned automatically on react
  • Three rank milestones at Level 1, 10, and 25
  • Realm-wide and individual leaderboards
  • XP cooldown (60s) prevents spam — paces naturally
  • XP per message: 5–25 (15 base ± 10 variance)
  • Level caps scale per wave (base: 50 member / 30 realm)

🎙️ Voice XP Core

Earn XP just by hanging out in voice channels. No commands needed — just be present with at least one other person.

  • 5 XP per tick (every 5 minutes) while in voice
  • Minimum 2 non-bot members required in the channel
  • AFK channel and self-deafened users are excluded
  • Streak and item XP buffs apply to voice XP too

🔥 Streak System Core

Stay active daily and earn escalating XP bonuses. Streaks reward consistency without punishing breaks too harshly.

  • 7 days → 3% XP bonus
  • 14 days → 6% XP bonus
  • 21 days → 9% XP bonus
  • 28 days → 12% XP bonus (cap)
  • Check your streak anytime with /streak

🎒 Items & Inventory Drops

Items drop randomly from messages. Every item belongs to the realm — not the individual. Realms build a shared stash together.

  • Universal items any realm can find
  • Realm-specific items unique to each path (6 per realm)
  • Rarity tiers: Common (2.5%), Uncommon (1.2%), Rare (0.4%), Legendary (0.08%)
  • Usable items with active effects (XP buffs, reveals, etc.)
  • Gift items between realms or trade across borders
  • Full item activity log for transparency

🔨 Crafting New

Combine common and uncommon materials from your realm's inventory into powerful usable items. Each realm has its own recipes plus universal ones.

  • View available recipes with /recipes
  • Craft items with /craft
  • Realm members get an objection window before crafting completes
  • Universal recipes: Realm Compass, Void Lantern, Worldseed
  • Realm recipes: rare + legendary items from common/uncommon materials

⚔️ Realm Battles Event

Events trigger randomly from messages (5% chance, 1hr cooldown) or can be started by admins. Six battle types keep things fresh.

  • ⚡ Riddle Battles — first correct answer wins XP for their realm
  • 🏃 Reaction Races — fastest finger on the right emoji wins
  • 🔎 Item Hunts — first realm to find a target item claims the prize
  • 🔤 Word Scrambles — unscramble a word with a hint for realm XP
  • 🔥 XP Frenzies — 10-minute 2x XP window, winning realm gets bonus
  • 🔗 Emoji Chains — react in the correct sequence of 5 emojis

🏴 Realm Raids PvP

Launch a raid against another realm to steal their items. Power is based on participant levels, reaction count, and a dash of randomness.

  • Start a raid with /raid
  • Both sides rally members via reactions
  • Power = (avg level × 1.5) + (participants × 1.0) + random factor
  • Winners steal 3 items from the defending realm
  • 2-hour cooldown between raids
  • XP rewards for both sides (400 win / 100 lose)

📋 Quests Daily / Weekly

Quests rotate automatically — daily and weekly targets that reward bonus realm XP on completion.

  • Message count targets (e.g. "Send 50 messages today")
  • XP milestones (e.g. "Earn 500 XP today")
  • Item collection goals (e.g. "Find 2 items today")
  • Seasonal quests during active seasons
  • Progress tracked automatically from normal chatting

📜 Lore, Waves & Seasons

The world that grows with you

📖 Realm Lore Story

Each realm has its own Book of Lore — a persistent page that updates live as new content is discovered.

  • 5 chapters per realm per wave, unlocking at realm level milestones
  • 3 lore fragments per realm per wave (uncommon / rare / legendary drops)
  • Fragment drop rate: 1% per XP-eligible message
  • Each realm has its own dedicated lore channel
  • Lore pages auto-update when chapters unlock or fragments are found

🌀 Wave System Progression

Lore is organized into waves — each wave is a full set of chapters and fragments for every realm. When all realms complete a wave, the Convergence fires.

  • Wave 1: Origins — "Where it all began."
  • Wave 2: Echoes — "What the past left behind."
  • Convergence resets realm progress and begins the next wave
  • Lore from past waves is preserved in wave history
  • Level caps increase with each wave (+50 member / +10 realm)
  • Track progress with /wave and /wave-history

🌸 Seasonal System Rotating

Four seasons, each tied to a realm. During a realm's season, exclusive content appears.

  • 🌱 Spring (Mar–May) — Grove of Growth
  • ☀️ Summer (Jun–Aug) — Ember Circle
  • 🍂 Autumn (Sep–Nov) — Mirror Lake
  • ❄️ Winter (Dec–Feb) — Moonroot Tree
  • 3 exclusive seasonal items per season (only drop during that window)
  • Seasonal quests with bonus XP rewards

🔮 Seasonal Fusion Endgame

Collect all 3 seasonal items during a season to forge a powerful fused artifact with game-changing effects.

  • 🌱 Verdant Heart — 3x XP for 1 hour + 1,000 bonus XP
  • ☀️ Eternal Ember — steal 2 items from each rival + 1,500 XP
  • 🍂 Veil of Echoes — copy 2 items from each rival + 2x drops for 1 hour
  • ❄️ Moonbound Relic — +500 XP to all realms, +2,000 to yours
  • Forge with /forge during the active season

📘 How to Use

Quick gameplay flow

1) Join a Realm

Pick a realm by reacting on the realm-selection message.

  • React with 🌿 🔥 💧 or 🌙 to join
  • EchoBloom assigns your realm role automatically
  • One realm at a time — unreact to leave
  • Non-realm emoji are blocked on the selection message

2) Chat & Progress

Just talk. Every message earns XP, drops items, and advances quests.

  • XP earned for you and your realm (5–25 per message)
  • Voice chat earns XP too (5 per 5-min tick)
  • Items drop randomly as you chat
  • Lore fragments surface from conversation (1% chance)
  • Quests and streaks track automatically in the background

3) Track Progress

Check your realm, your rank, and how your realm stacks up.

  • /realm — your level, XP, rank, and progress bar
  • /level — detailed stats (yours or another user's)
  • /streak — your daily streak and XP bonus
  • /realms — all realms with levels and member counts
  • /leaderboard — top members in a realm
  • /wave — current wave progress across all realms

4) Engage with Systems

Items, crafting, raids, quests, lore, events, and seasons all layer on top of chat.

  • /inventory — view your realm's shared stash
  • /item-info — see what an item does
  • /use — activate a usable item
  • /buffs — view active realm XP buffs
  • /recipes and /craft — crafting system
  • /gift and /trade — move items between realms
  • /raid — attack another realm for their items
  • /forge — fuse seasonal items into artifacts
  • /quests — view active daily/weekly/seasonal quests
  • /lore — view your realm's story progress
  • /season — current season info and exclusive content

🔧 Setup Notes

Start clean, keep it readable
Recommended rollout

Enable realms first. Once people are chatting and earning XP, the items, quests, events, lore, and seasons all layer in naturally. No need to introduce everything at once. Crafting, raids, and seasonal fusion add depth for established communities.

Setup flow: 1. Create realm roles (one per realm) 2. Create rank roles (3 per realm: Level 1, 10, 25) 3. Fill in role IDs in config/config.py 4. Run /setup-realms in your realm-select channel 5. Create lore channels (one per realm) and run /setup-lore 6. Set your events channel ID in config 7. Run /setup-events to post the persistent dashboard 8. Restart — quests auto-rotate, events trigger from chat 9. Run /season to verify seasonal content is active 10. Run /wave to check lore wave status
Everything is in one file

All IDs, realm definitions, items, crafting recipes, quests, lore waves, seasonal content, riddles, word scrambles, emoji chains, and drop rates live in config/config.py. One file to rule them all.
