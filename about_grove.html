🌱 Realm Ranks

Growth earned through presence

Members of the Grove earn XP through conversation. As they level up, they earn rank roles that mark their journey through the forest.

• Rank 1 (Level 1) — You've planted your first root.
• Rank 2 (Level 10) — The canopy recognizes you.
• Rank 3 (Level 25) — You are part of the Grove itself.

🎒 Realm Items

What the Grove discovers

Items drop randomly from messages and belong to the realm's shared inventory. Grove members can find universal items plus these realm-specific drops:

🍃 Verdant Leaf (Common) — Still green, still growing.
🪨 Rootstone (Uncommon) — Heavy with patience.
🌳 Living Branch (Rare) — A branch that still sprouts.

🌱 Season of Growth

Spring (March – May)

The Grove awakens. Life stirs beneath the soil.

During Spring, exclusive seasonal items can only be found by chatting. These items disappear from the drop pool when the season ends — making them true limited collectibles.

👑 Seedling Crown (Uncommon) — Woven from the first sprouts of the year.
💧 Dewdrop Charm (Rare) — Morning light trapped in a single drop.

📖 Book of Lore

Chapters unlock as the realm levels up

These chapters are revealed in Discord as the Grove reaches each milestone. The bot's persistent lore page updates live when a new chapter unlocks.

Chapter I: The First Root

Unlocks at Realm Level 3

Before the Grove was a grove, there was a single root — pale and searching — that broke through stone to find the light. It did not know what it was becoming. It only knew it had to grow.

Chapter II: The Canopy Whisper

Unlocks at Realm Level 7

As the Grove grew tall, its leaves began to whisper to one another. Not words, but intention. The trees learned that growth shared is growth multiplied. They reached for each other, and the canopy closed.

Chapter III: The Hollow Heart

Unlocks at Realm Level 12

Deep in the oldest trunk, a hollow formed — not from decay, but from trust. The Grove had learned to hold space. To be strong and empty at once. Creatures came to rest there, and left changed.

Chapter IV: The Overgrowth

Unlocks at Realm Level 18

Growth unchecked becomes a tangle. The Grove nearly lost itself, vines choking vines, roots fighting roots. But the oldest tree spoke without speaking: 'Not more. Better.' And the Grove remembered its purpose.

Chapter V: The Eternal Bloom

Unlocks at Realm Level 25

In the deepest glade, a flower blooms that never wilts. It has no name. It needs no water. It exists because the Grove exists — a living proof that patience, given enough time, becomes something permanent.
