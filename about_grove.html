📜 Grove Lore

The realm that remembers every kindness

Long before the realms split apart, the Grove was the heartwood of the world — the place where all magic returned to rest.

The Grove does not chase glory. It does not burn bright or shimmer strange. Instead, it grows quietly beneath everything: roots beneath cities, vines around ruins, moss on forgotten stone.

Grove-bound souls are builders, healers, caretakers, and guardians. Their power comes from patience — and when the Grove awakens, it is less a sudden explosion… and more an unstoppable spring.
