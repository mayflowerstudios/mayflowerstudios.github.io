🔐 Steelhold

Steelhold adds secure, stylish safes to your world — built for servers, collectors, and anyone who wants their valuables protected. Claim ownership, lock it down, and store your gear with confidence.

📦 Download Steelhold 📜 Changelog

✨ Description

Steelhold is a security-focused storage mod centered on safes you can place, claim, and manage. It’s designed to feel “vanilla-friendly” in survival while still giving you real protection on multiplayer servers.

  • Secure storage: safes are meant to protect items from casual access.
  • Ownership-based: the safe remembers who owns it.
  • Server-friendly: great for bases, shops, factions, and shared worlds.
  • Multiple safe types: use whatever variant fits your style and progression.
Tip: If you plan to run Steelhold on a server, keep your “claim / key” rules consistent so players instantly understand what’s allowed.

🧭 How to Use

Here’s the typical Steelhold flow once it’s installed:

  1. Craft a safe (see crafting below).
  2. Place it where you want it protected.
  3. Claim / set ownership (first interaction usually assigns the owner).
  4. Open it and store items like a normal container.
  5. Use keys (if enabled) to grant access to trusted players without giving them everything else.
