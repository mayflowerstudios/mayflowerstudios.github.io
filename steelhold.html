📝 Description

What Steelhold is

Steelhold adds secure, survival-balanced storage built for real multiplayer worlds. Place safes, claim ownership, and control access through a key-style system that’s designed to reduce grief without turning storage into an overpowered “magic lock.”

Designed for servers

Clean interactions, predictable behavior, and a “don’t break long-running worlds” mindset.

📘 How to Use

Quick gameplay guide

1) Place & Claim

Set down a Steelhold safe and it becomes yours.

  • Place the safe like any block
  • First owner interaction claims the safe
  • Owner can configure access (depending on the safe type)

2) Access & Keys

Share access without sharing your base.

  • Owner can create/give keys (or authorize players)
  • Keys allow trusted friends to open the safe
  • Use this for teams, shops, or shared vaults

3) Moving Safes

Keep your world safe from “oops it deleted.”

  • Recommended: empty safes before moving/breaking
  • On servers: keep claims/keys consistent with your rules
  • Back up before major upgrades (always good practice)

4) Config & Tuning

Adjust balance to match your server.

  • Launch once to generate config
  • Tune values for difficulty / access rules
  • Restart after changing config settings
