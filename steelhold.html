📝 Description

What Steelhold is

Steelhold is a Forge mod that adds heavy-duty, lockable safes to Minecraft — built for survival, multiplayer, PvP, and roleplay servers where security actually matters.

These aren’t decorative chests.
Steelhold safes are physical vaults with real ownership, real locks, and real consequences.

🗄️ Secure Storage, Not Just More Slots

Every Steelhold safe is player-owned, key-locked, explosion-resistant, tool-restricted, and physics-enabled.

Safes behave like anvils — if the block underneath breaks, they fall. When they land, they keep their inventory, owner, and lock intact, making them perfect for traps, vaults, and high-security bases.

🔑 Lock & Key System

When a safe is placed, it automatically binds to its owner. Only the owner can open it, generate keys, or share access. Keys are physical items bound to a specific safe — tradable, and impossible to fake or duplicate.

No key? No access.

Even operators must break a safe to get inside — there are no bypasses.

🧱 Multiple Tiers

  • Basic Safe – Starter protection
  • Reinforced Safe – Stronger and blast-resistant
  • Steelhold Safe – High-security vault
  • Vault Safe – Massive storage
  • Bank Safe – Maximum security & capacity

💥 Explosions, Tools & Config

Safes require the correct pickaxe tier to break and resist TNT/creepers based on tier. Everything is configurable: storage size, hardness, blast resistance, and required tool tier. Existing safes are never shrunk, preventing item loss.

Built for servers

No hopper theft. No chest breaking. No free loot from TNT.
If someone wants what’s inside, they have to earn it.

📘 How to Use

Quick gameplay guide

1) Place & Claim

Set down a Steelhold safe and it becomes yours.

  • Place the safe like any block
  • First owner interaction claims the safe
  • Owner can configure access (depending on the safe type)

2) Access & Keys

Share access without sharing your base.

  • Owner can create/give keys (or authorize players)
  • Keys allow trusted friends to open the safe
  • Use this for teams, shops, or shared vaults

3) Moving Safes

Keep your world safe from “oops it deleted.”

  • Recommended: empty safes before moving/breaking
  • On servers: keep claims/keys consistent with your rules
  • Back up before major upgrades (always good practice)

4) Config & Tuning

Adjust balance to match your server.

  • Launch once to generate config
  • Tune values for difficulty / access rules
  • Restart after changing config settings
