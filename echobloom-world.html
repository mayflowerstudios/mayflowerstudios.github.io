📊 Drop Rates

How items find their way into your realm's inventory

💬 Chat & Voice Drops

Every XP-eligible message has a chance to drop an item. Rates below are per message, before class bonuses.

Rarity Base chance With Trickster Lv 30
Common 2.5% ~3.9%
Uncommon 1.5% ~2.3%
Rare 0.7% ~1.1%
Legendary 0.2% ~0.3%

Trickster at Lv 30 stacks +55% item drop rate (Passive + Five-Finger Discount + Con Artist). Wanderer Lv 30 adds a further +35%.

⚗️ Crafting Notes
  • Legendary recipes have a 70% success rate — materials are consumed on failure
  • Scholar's Studied Hand (+10%) and Oracle's Omniscience (+15%) boost success rates
  • Realm members get an objection window after you start a craft — majority vote can cancel it
  • Crafted items go directly into the realm's shared inventory

3× XP for your realm for 1 hour + 1,000 instant bonus XP.

The pulse of spring condensed into a single heartbeat. The strongest XP burst in the game — use it during peak activity hours.

A flame that remembers every fire before it. Hits all three rivals at once — the most aggressive item in the game.

A mirror that reflects possibility. Unlike Eternal Ember, rivals keep their items — but you still get copies from all three.

+500 XP to ALL realms, then +2,000 additional bonus XP to yours.

A relic dreamed into existence. The most cooperative fusion — lifts every realm while giving yours a massive lead.

🐉 Server Bosses

9 bosses · HP scales per fighter · all participants earn class XP on defeat · MVP gets a guaranteed loot drop
How boss HP scales

Total HP = base_hp + (hp_per_fighter × number of fighters). A boss with base 320 HP and +80/fighter has 480 HP with 2 fighters, 640 with 4, and 960 with 8. Join early — every new participant increases the shared HP pool.
