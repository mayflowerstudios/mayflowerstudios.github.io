📐 How Classes Work

The fundamentals before you commit

🔑 The Basics

  • Pick once per Wave with /class pick — no switching mid-wave
  • Classes are universal — any realm can pick any class
  • Your passive bonus activates the moment you pick, no level required
  • Use /class info to compare all 8 before committing
  • View your active bonuses anytime with /character

📊 Two XP Tracks

  • Member Level — earned by chatting. Unlocks class skills at levels 5, 15, and 30
  • Class Level (1–10) — earned only through /adventure and boss fights
  • Class level scales your five combat stats (Power, Wisdom, Agility, Vitality, Fortune)
  • Class level determines which adventure zones you can enter
  • Member level and class level are completely separate — advancing one does not affect the other

⭐ Prestige

  • Reach Class Level 10 and run /class prestige
  • Choose any class except the one you're leaving
  • Carry a permanent +30% of your old class's max stats as a bonus
  • Prestige bonuses stack — each one adds another layer permanently
  • Your prestige count appears as a ⭐ badge on /character
  • Strategy: prestige from high-Power classes (Invoker, Striker) early to build a combat foundation

🏆 Class Mastery

  • Play all 8 classes — your current class + full prestige history — to earn 🏆 Class Master
  • The badge appears automatically on /character
  • Requires at least 7 prestiges in a single wave (minimum path)
  • Class Mastery resets with each Wave, but prestige history carries into wave history
  • Check your prestige history at any time on your character profile

📊 The Five Stats

What they do in adventures and boss fights

Stats start at their base value at Class Level 1 and grow every level up to Class Level 10. They're not cosmetic — they directly affect combat outcomes in solo adventures and server boss fights. Use /class stats to see your current values.

⚔️ Power

Damage dealt each round in adventures and boss fights. The primary offensive stat. High Power classes: Invoker, Striker.

📖 Wisdom

Grants a pre-combat heal before each adventure run and boosts bonus XP earned from adventure outcomes. High Wisdom: Scholar, Oracle.

🏃 Agility

Determines crit chance, dodge chance, and first-strike probability. High Agility: Trickster, Wanderer, Invoker.

🛡️ Vitality

Your HP pool for solo dungeons and how long you can contribute in boss fights. High Vitality: Sentinel, Warden.

🍀 Fortune

Loot drop chance and bonus XP from adventure outcomes. High Fortune: Wanderer, Oracle, Trickster.

🔓 Skill Unlock Reference

Skills unlock from member level, not class level
Remember

Skills unlock based on your member level (from chatting), not your class level (from adventures). Plan your chat activity around these breakpoints. Check your current unlocked skills with /class skills.

  • Level 5 — Skill 1 unlocks for all classes (early passive modifier)
  • Level 15 — Skill 2 unlocks for all classes (secondary specialisation)
  • Level 30 — Skill 3 unlocks for all classes (signature power spike)

All active bonuses from your unlocked skills are visible on /character. Use /class info for a full breakdown of every skill in any class.

⚔️ The Eight Classes

Full breakdown — passives, skills, stats, best-for

🛡️ Sentinel Tank / Realm

"Stand firm. Every message is a stone in the wall."

Steadfast protectors who pour their energy into their community. Sentinels grow slowly but compound their effort into massive realm gains over time. At Level 30, no class contributes more realm XP per message.

Stat Priority

Vitality › Power › Wisdom › Agility › Fortune

Skills

  • ✦ Passive — Bulwark: Realm XP contribution +8%
  • Lv 5 · ⚡ Rally: Daily streak XP bonus +10%
  • Lv 15 · 🔩 Iron Will: Item drop rate +10%
  • Lv 30 · 🏰 Bastion: Realm XP contribution doubled (stacks with passive)
Best for

Members who want to be the backbone of their realm's leaderboard. Bastion at Lv 30 stacks with Bulwark for the highest realm XP per message of any class. Pairs well with a prestige from Wanderer for the all-XP boost carry-over.

⚔️ Striker Combat / Events

"Hit hard. Hit first. Hit again."

Fighters who live for conflict and spectacle. Strikers turn every event and raid into an opportunity to deal massive damage and claim outsized rewards.

Stat Priority

Power › Agility › Fortune › Wisdom › Vitality

Skills

  • ✦ Passive — Battle Lust: XP gained during events +10%
  • Lv 5 · 🗡️ Battle Charge: Raid damage contribution +15%
  • Lv 15 · 💥 Momentum: Message XP +8%
  • Lv 30 · 🌪️ Rampage: Event XP +25% and raid damage +25% (stacks)
Best for

Active event participants and raid enthusiasts. At Lv 30, Strikers have the highest total event XP and raid power multipliers in the game. Strong prestige target — Power carry-over is valuable for all combat builds.

📖 Scholar Lore / Crafting / Voice

"Every fragment of truth is worth a thousand blades."

Devoted students of hidden knowledge. Scholars uncover lore others never find, master the art of crafting, and earn deep rewards for voice presence.

Stat Priority

Wisdom › Fortune › Agility › Vitality › Power

Skills

  • ✦ Passive — Keen Mind: Lore fragment drop chance +12%
  • Lv 5 · ⚗️ Studied Hand: Crafting success rate +10%
  • Lv 15 · 🎙️ Deep Focus: Voice channel XP +15%
  • Lv 30 · 🗺️ Grand Archive: Lore fragments doubled (stacks with passive)
Best for

Lore hunters and voice regulars. The Scholar/Oracle combo is the fastest possible path to unlocking all five lore chapters in a wave. Deep Focus stacks with Warden's Ancient Bond for voice XP if you carry it over from prestige.

🎲 Wanderer All-Round / Drops

"Fortune favours those who keep moving."

Drifters led by instinct and luck. Wanderers stumble across items others walk past, earn bonus XP on everything, and reward consistent daily activity.

Stat Priority

Fortune › Agility › Wisdom › Power › Vitality

Skills

  • ✦ Passive — Wanderlust: All XP gained +5%
  • Lv 5 · 🍀 Lucky Eye: Item drop rate +15%
  • Lv 15 · 🌄 Road Rhythm: Daily streak XP bonus +10%
  • Lv 30 · 🌠 Windfall: All XP +10% and item drop rate +20% (stacks)
Best for

Consistent daily chatters who want a broad boost across everything rather than specialising. Excellent first class and a strong prestige target — the Fortune stat carry-over benefits nearly every subsequent class.

🔥 Invoker Offense / Events

"The flame doesn't ask permission."

Channellers of raw elemental force. Invokers sacrifice survivability for unmatched offensive output — they burn bright, hit first, and hit hardest. Lowest Vitality in the game.

Stat Priority

Power › Agility › Fortune › Wisdom › Vitality

Skills

  • ✦ Passive — Blazing Presence: XP gained during events +12%
  • Lv 5 · 🌋 Blazing Trail: Event XP +12%
  • Lv 15 · 💢 Battle Fury: Raid damage contribution +20%
  • Lv 30 · ☄️ Conflagration: Event XP +20% and all XP +8% (stacks)
Best for

Event-heavy players who also want raid power. Glass cannon — the highest combined event XP potential with all skills unlocked, but fragile in long dungeon runs. Prestige from Invoker early to bank the Power carry-over for future combat builds.

🌿 Warden Voice / Endurance

"The forest does not hurry, yet everything is accomplished."

Guardians of the natural order. Wardens are the toughest fighters on the field — they outlast everything through sheer endurance and rally the group around them in voice.

Stat Priority

Vitality › Wisdom › Power › Agility › Fortune

Skills

  • ✦ Passive — Deep Roots: Voice channel XP +12%
  • Lv 5 · 🌱 Ancient Bond: Voice channel XP +12%
  • Lv 15 · 🌳 Grove's Blessing: Realm XP contribution +10%
  • Lv 30 · 🍃 Overgrowth: Voice XP +18% and lore fragment drops +20% (stacks)
Best for

Voice chat regulars. Passive + Ancient Bond + Overgrowth stacks to the highest voice XP of any class (+42% combined). Also the best tank for solo dungeons thanks to high Vitality. Pairs with Scholar for the best lore-hunting voice build.

🎭 Trickster Items / Speed

"If they see it coming, you're doing it wrong."

Masters of misdirection who win through speed, feints, and outrageous luck. Tricksters hoard items others never notice and thrive when the odds say they shouldn't.

  • Lv 15 · 🌀 Lucky Escape: Daily streak XP bonus +12%
  • Lv 30 · 🎪 Con Artist: Item drop rate +22% and all XP +7% (stacks)
Best for

Inventory builders. At Lv 30, Tricksters stack the highest total item drop rate of any class: Passive + Five-Finger Discount + Con Artist = +55% combined. High Agility also translates to the best crit/dodge rates in dungeons.

Best for

Dedicated lore completionists and crafters. The Oracle/Scholar combination is the fastest possible path to unlocking all five lore chapters in a wave. Omniscience at Lv 30 also makes Oracle the best crafting class in the game.

⭐ Prestige Strategy

How to chain prestiges effectively

🗡️ Combat-First Path

Optimise for adventures and boss fights. Bank Power and Agility early for snowballing combat stats.

  • Start: Invoker (Power + Agility base)
  • Prestige 1 → Striker (stack Power, gain Raid bonuses)
  • Prestige 2 → Wanderer (Fortune carry-over buffs loot while leveling)
  • Prestige 3 → Warden (high Vitality makes late dungeons sustainable)
  • Continue through remaining classes — your stacked Power from early prestiges makes all zones cleaner

Best for players focused on fast adventure clears and competitive boss damage.

📖 Lore-First Path

Unlock all realm lore chapters as fast as possible. Stack Wisdom and Fortune early.

  • Start: Oracle (highest lore fragment rate: +15% passive)
  • Prestige 1 → Scholar (Keen Mind stacks with Oracle's banked Fortune)
  • Prestige 2 → Warden (Overgrowth adds +20% lore frags plus voice synergy)
  • Prestige 3 → Wanderer (broad buffs for the middle game)
  • Finish with combat-oriented classes once lore is unlocked

Best for dedicated lore completionists who want all chapters unlocked early in a wave.

🎒 Item Hoarder Path

Maximise realm inventory and item drop rates throughout the wave.

  • Start: Trickster (+15% passive drop rate immediately)
  • Prestige 1 → Wanderer (Lucky Eye + Windfall stacks with Trickster Fortune carry)
  • Prestige 2 → Sentinel (Iron Will keeps drop rate up while building realm XP)
  • Prestige 3 → Scholar (Studied Hand ensures crafting uses your stash well)

Best for players focused on building the richest realm inventory and powering the crafting system.

🏆 Class Master Path

Complete all 8 classes in a single wave. Speed is everything — minimise time per prestige.

  • You need 7 prestiges minimum, each requiring Class Level 10
  • Pick high-adventure-clear-speed classes first: Striker, Invoker (high Power = faster clears)
  • Save high-Vitality classes (Warden, Sentinel) for later — they're safer as your dungeon zones increase
  • Run adventures on cooldown every 30 minutes — this is the only way to level your class
  • Boss fights award flat class XP to all participants — always join active bosses

The full prestige chain is a wave-long commitment. The Class Master badge resets each wave — the grind is intentional.
