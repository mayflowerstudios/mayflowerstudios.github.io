🧭 Eras

Eras are the server’s “chapters.” You unlock them by time lived + crafting a milestone. Unlocking an era expands what blocks/items you can use — and some eras are parallel choices.

Last updated: — Eras: —

📖 How it works

Player-friendly
  • Time gate: some eras require a certain amount of playtime.
  • Milestone craft: once eligible, craft one milestone item for that era to unlock it.
  • Choice-based: eras can be unlocked in parallel — you don’t have to take every path.
  • Gates: each era can restrict blocks/items until it’s unlocked.
In‑game commands
/era next — shows what’s ready / coming soon
/era <id> — shows the milestone + requirements for a specific era
