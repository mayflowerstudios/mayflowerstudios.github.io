📝 What it does

The whole chain, added up

Craft Planner answers one question properly: what do I actually need to go and get. Choose the item, choose how many, and it follows every recipe down to the things nothing else makes, then adds them all up.

Adding up matters more than it sounds. When two branches of a craft both want the same ingredient, and the recipe for it makes more than one at a time, rounding each branch on its own asks you for more than you need. Everything is totalled across the whole tree first and only then turned into whole crafts, so the numbers are the smallest ones that really work — and the spare that comes out at the end is listed too.

Item names are read from your own Endless Online folder, so they stay right after a game update. A copy is built into the app for anyone who has the game somewhere unusual.

The recipes themselves are not in the game files — the server sends them when you open a craft NPC — so they are typed in. That is why they are shared: once one person writes a recipe down, nobody else has to.
