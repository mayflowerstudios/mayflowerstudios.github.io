🌿 What it does

A whole radio station in one window

🎵

Music from links

Paste a YouTube video or playlist, or a Spotify song, album, or playlist — the app builds your library for you. Drag & drop your own MP3s too.

🎚️

Real DJ controls

Shuffle, repeat, pause, seek, and an up-next queue. Listeners hear one continuous, gapless stream — with the current song title showing in players.

🛜

Sets itself up

First run downloads everything it needs and asks your router to open the port automatically. A status pill tells you the moment the world can hear you.

✨

Stays fresh

When there's a new version, an update button appears — one click and the app updates itself. No reinstalling, ever.

🚀 Getting started

Three minutes to on-air

Download and run

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the app is from a small studio — click More info → Run anyway. First-time setup runs by itself and takes a few minutes.

Allow the firewall popup

When the radio server starts the first time, Windows asks about icecast.exe — click Allow so listeners can reach you.

Add music and go live

Paste a link, press Play, and copy your radio URL from the top of the app into wherever you want it heard. When the Internet pill is green, everyone can listen.

The radio runs from your PC — keep the app open while you're broadcasting, and the stream stops when you close it. If your router doesn't support automatic port opening (UPnP), the app shows exactly what to set up instead.

Please be thoughtful about what you broadcast and to whom — you're responsible for your own station. 🌸
