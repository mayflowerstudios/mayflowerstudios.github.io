🌿 About Mayflower Studios

How we build

Mayflower Studios is an independent development studio creating imaginative projects across games, mods, interactive tools, and community-driven experiences. We care about polish, reliability, and building things people genuinely enjoy — not just things that technically function.

Our philosophy is simple:
• Meaningful experiences (projects with charm and purpose)
• Clean, thoughtful design (systems that feel good to use)
• Long-term creativity (worlds, stories, and tools that grow over time)
• Community-first development (built for players, creators, and shared spaces)
