🧱 Minecraft Mods

Survival-friendly • Server-ready • Built to last

Our Minecraft mods aim for the “vanilla+ sweet spot” — systems that feel natural in survival while still solving real problems. That means clean interfaces, predictable behavior, and server-tunable balance.

Steelhold focuses on security: player-owned safes, lock/key access, tier progression, and physical “heavy vault” behavior. LogiChest focuses on organization: hub routing, sorter whitelists, and upgrade-driven automation that stays readable even as your base grows.

If you like tidy bases and reliable mechanics, you’re in the right place.

🤖 Discord Bots

Private bots built specifically for our community

Our bots are built for a single home: our server. They aren’t public invite bots — they’re the “infrastructure” of our community.

FoxBot is the cozy companion: birthdays, reaction roles, private chat rooms, tone-based replies, and moderation guardrails. EchoBloom is the living-world layer: realms, adventure commands, realm inventory, craftable buffs, raids, reaction battles, and seasonal story systems.

They’re designed to be fun without being spammy, and powerful without being hard to maintain.

🌿 About Mayflower Studios

How we build

Mayflower Studios is an independent dev studio creating tools and experiences across Minecraft and Discord. We care about polish, reliability, and making projects that people actually enjoy using — not just things that technically work.

Our design philosophy:
• Readable systems (no spaghetti logic)
• Survival-friendly pacing (fun loops, not grind walls)
• Server-minded stability (long-running worlds, real communities)
• Config where it matters (tune balance, don’t rewrite mods)

Projects evolve over time — refined, expanded, and improved as we learn what works in real servers and real worlds.
