🧱 Minecraft Mods

Survival-friendly. Server-ready. Built to last.

Our Minecraft mods are designed to feel natural in survival worlds — clean interfaces, simple interactions, and systems that reward organization without turning your base into a science project.

We prioritize multiplayer stability, performance, and configurability so servers can run the same worlds for months without chaos. Whether it’s secure storage, smart routing, or quality-of-life systems, we aim for mods that scale from singleplayer builds to busy community servers.

If you like tidy bases and reliable automation, you’re in the right place.

🤖 Discord Bots

FoxBot • EchoBloom

Our Discord bots range from playful community companions to full progression systems built around lore, events, and long-term engagement. Some are designed to add charm and personality to your server — others become the backbone of a living world with realms, ranks, and seasonal content.

Everything is built for real servers: configurable settings, moderation hooks, quality-of-life features, and careful handling so bots stay stable even when chat gets busy. The goal is always the same — features that feel fun to use, not annoying to manage.

Whether you want cozy interaction or a full realm-based progression experience, our bots are made to grow with your community.

🌿 About Mayflower Studios

How we build

Mayflower Studios is an independent dev studio building tools and experiences across Minecraft, Discord, and beyond. We care about polish, reliability, and making projects that people actually enjoy using — not just things that technically work.

We focus on clean design, stable systems, and long-term maintainability. That means sensible defaults, real configuration options, and features that behave well in multiplayer and real community environments.

Our projects are built to evolve over time — improved, expanded, and refined as they grow with the people using them.
