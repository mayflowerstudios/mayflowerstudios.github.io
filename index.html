About the Studio

Mayflower Studios is an independent development studio creating imaginative projects across games, mods, interactive tools, and community-driven experiences. We care deeply about polish, reliability, and building things people genuinely enjoy using every day.

Every project starts with a question: "Would I actually want to play this?" That keeps us honest, keeps scope reasonable, and makes everything a little more magical.
