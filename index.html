🧱 Minecraft Mods

Steelhold • LogiChest

Our Minecraft mods are built with survival gameplay in mind, focusing on systems that feel natural to use and fit seamlessly into existing worlds. We prioritize clean, intuitive interfaces and mechanics that respect the vanilla experience while still offering meaningful upgrades.

Every mod is designed to be server-ready and long-term stable, with multiplayer performance, compatibility, and reliability at the forefront. Whether it’s secure storage, automation, or quality-of-life improvements, we build mods that scale well from small private worlds to active community servers.

If you enjoy tidy bases, organized systems, and mods that “just work” without unnecessary complexity, Mayflower Studios’ Minecraft projects are built with you in mind.

🤖 Discord Bots

FoxBot • EchoBloom

Our Discord bots range from playful community companions to fully featured progression systems built around lore, events, and long-term engagement. Some are designed to add charm and personality to a server, while others form the backbone of evolving communities with ranks, realms, and seasonal systems.

Every bot is built for real, active servers — with configurable features, moderation hooks, and thoughtful quality-of-life tools that make day-to-day management easier. We focus on reliability, clarity, and flexibility, ensuring bots can scale from small friend groups to large, lore-driven communities without becoming brittle or overwhelming.

Whether it’s lighthearted interaction, structured progression, or immersive world-building, our goal is to create bots that feel alive, purposeful, and rewarding to interact with over time.

🌿 About Mayflower Studios

Who we are & how we build

Mayflower Studios is an independent development studio creating tools and experiences across multiple platforms, including Minecraft, Discord, and custom software projects. We focus on building systems that are reliable, well-designed, and enjoyable to use — whether they’re powering online communities or enhancing in-game experiences.

Our work emphasizes polish, long-term stability, and thoughtful design. We care about how things feel to use just as much as how they function, paying attention to the details that turn a project from “working” into something people genuinely enjoy.

From community-driven bots to immersive game mods and experimental software ideas, Mayflower Studios is driven by a simple goal: create projects people want to come back to — and keep improving them over time.
