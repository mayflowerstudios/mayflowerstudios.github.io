🌿 About Mayflower Studios

Short studio blurb

Mayflower Studios is an independent development studio creating tools and experiences across multiple platforms, including Minecraft, Discord, and custom software projects. We focus on building systems that are reliable, well-designed, and enjoyable to use, whether they’re powering online communities or enhancing in-game experiences.

Our work emphasizes polish, long-term stability, and thoughtful design. We care about how things feel to use just as much as how they function, paying attention to the small details that turn a project from “working” into something people actually love interacting with.

From community-driven bots to immersive game mods and experimental software ideas, Mayflower Studios is driven by a simple goal: create projects that people enjoy returning to, and that continue to grow and improve over time.
