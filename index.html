🧱 Minecraft Mods

Survival-friendly • Server-ready • Built to last

Our Minecraft mods are designed to feel natural in survival worlds — clean interfaces, simple interactions, and systems that reward organization without turning your base into a science project.

We prioritize multiplayer stability, performance, and configurability so servers can run the same worlds for months without chaos. Whether it’s secure storage, smart routing, or quality-of-life systems, we aim for mods that scale from singleplayer builds to busy community servers.

If you like tidy bases and reliable automation, you’re in the right place.

🌿 About Mayflower Studios

How we build

Mayflower Studios is an independent dev studio creating tools and experiences across Minecraft, Discord, and beyond. We care about polish, reliability, and making projects that people actually enjoy using — not just things that technically work.

We focus on clean design, stable systems, and long-term maintainability. That means sensible defaults, real configuration options, and features that behave well in multiplayer and real community environments.

Our projects are built to evolve over time — improved, expanded, and refined as they grow with the people using them.
