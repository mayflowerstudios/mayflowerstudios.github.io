✅ The Cozy Contract

The rules behind the vibe

This is a Cozy SurvivalRP server. That means we’re here for long-term builds, slow progression, and shared world storytelling.

  1. Progress slowly. The server is era-gated. Don’t try to bypass progression.
  2. Build with care. This world is meant to feel lived-in, not disposable.
  3. Vibes over domination. Don’t treat the server like a warzone.
  4. Respect the gates. If something is locked, it’s locked for a reason.
  5. Protect the story. Your choices affect the whole community.

If you follow that mindset, you’ll basically never get bonked by staff. ✨

🌿 Eras & Progression

Time + milestones = your story unlocking

This server uses an Era System. Content unlocks through time lived and milestone crafting. Some paths are parallel — your journey is your own.

✅ Allowed

  • Progressing efficiently (within the rules).
  • Choosing specializations (magic, industry, guns, etc.).
  • Letting your base/theme reflect your current era.
  • Trading with players in your era (or earlier).

🚫 Not Allowed

  • Bypassing gates via exploits, loopholes, or weird edge cases.
  • “Boosting” new players with late-era items to skip progression.
  • AFK abuse to farm time (it won’t help, and it’s not the vibe).

Tip: use /era next to see your unlock options and /era <id> to see milestones.

🏡 Builds, Claims, & World Integrity

Build cool things; don’t torch other people’s cool things
  • No griefing. (Lava, TNT, fire, explosions, withers, etc.) in or near someone’s build.
  • No theft. Don’t “borrow” from bases, chests, machines, vehicles, or farms.
  • If you find an unclaimed stash/structure, ask before taking.
  • Spawn is curated. Don’t clutter it with junk or trap builds.
  • Large builds are welcome — just don’t block paths, portals, or community areas.
Vibe rule: if it ruins immersion or the cozy aesthetic for others, it probably shouldn’t be there.

🔫 Combat, Guns, & “Toys”

Yes, the pack has toys. No, you can’t be a menace 24/7.
  • PvP is consent-based. Unless it’s a server event or designated arena, ask first.
  • No spawn camping. Don’t ambush new players or repeatedly target one person.
  • No random drive-bys. Shooting players or bases without consent counts as griefing.
  • Vehicles: don’t ram builds, block entrances, or park on top of someone’s stuff.
  • Events/arenas: rules may change per event — the event post becomes the rulebook.
Want “always-on PvP”? We can designate a region/arena where it’s automatically allowed.

⚙️ Performance & Automation

Help the server not melt
  • No lag machines. Redstone clocks, infinite loops, or spammy contraptions get removed.
  • Farms are fine — but keep them reasonable. If it tanks TPS, we’ll ask you to optimize it.
  • Chunkloaders: use sparingly. Don’t keep half the world loaded 24/7.
  • Mob caps: don’t build “spawn forever” grinders that cause entity floods.
  • If staff asks you to tweak a build for performance, please do it — we’ll help you.
This is a long-term world. Performance stability beats personal megafarms every time.

🌎 Community & Roleplay

We’re building history here

This is SurvivalRP — not hardcore roleplay, but the world should feel like a world.

  • You don’t have to stay “in character” 24/7.
  • But don’t intentionally break immersion constantly.
  • Build themed towns, taverns, farms, shops, guild halls, cozy homes, etc.
  • Participate in events when you can — they shape the server’s ongoing story.

You’re not just surviving — you’re shaping the timeline. 📚

💬 Chat & Conduct

Vibes matter
  • No hate speech, bigotry, slurs, or targeted harassment.
  • No doxxing, threats, or sharing private info.
  • Keep spam to a minimum (caps, repeated messages, mic spam if voice chat is used).
  • PG-13-ish is the goal. If you wouldn’t say it in a mixed group, don’t.

🧠 AFK & Time Progression

Time lived should mean time played

Progression uses time lived + milestones. AFK farming time won’t help and isn’t the vibe.

  • If you’re away, log out.
  • Don’t build contraptions meant to keep you “active”.
  • If staff flags a setup as AFK abuse, you’ll be asked to remove it.

Cozy servers thrive when progression stays fair. 🌿

🛠️ Enforcement

How we handle rule breaks

We’re chill, but we protect the world. Depending on severity, we usually follow this ladder:

  • Reminder / warning (with what to change)
  • Rollback / removal (grief, stolen items, lag builds)
  • Temporary mute/kick
  • Temporary ban
  • Permanent ban (repeat issues or severe behavior)

If something feels unfair, ping staff in Discord calmly. We’ll talk it out.

🧾 Quick FAQ

Stuff people always ask

Can I prank? Light pranks are okay if they’re reversible and not stressful. If it causes cleanup or panic, it’s griefing.

Can I raid bases? Not unless it’s a planned event with explicit rules.

Can I build big farms? Yes — just don’t lag the server. If it’s heavy, we’ll help optimize.

Can I speedrun tech? You can progress efficiently, but not by bypassing gating or boosting others past their era.

What about explosives? Use responsibly. No TNT/wither near builds, spawn, or community zones.
